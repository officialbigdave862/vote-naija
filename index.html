THE VISION

Technology should support trust,
not ask people to take it on faith.

SafeVote Nigeria is a concept for exploring how carefully governed technology could support electoral participation while protecting the rights and safety of voters.

♧

Designed for everyone

Clear navigation, readable text, keyboard support and a roadmap for assistive technologies and local-language access.

◉

Privacy at the centre

Explore how voter eligibility can be separated from ballot choices, with strict data minimization and privacy review.

⌁

Auditable by design

Plan independent testing, documented procedures, accessible complaints channels and evidence-based review of results.

THE CONCEPT

A careful process.
Clear safeguards.

A possible workflow for a future, legally authorized system. These steps are illustrative and are not implemented as a real election service.

01
▤

Confirm eligibility

Use an approved, privacy-preserving method to verify eligibility—only after legal and technical review.

02
▣

Review the ballot

Present candidates and instructions in an accessible, neutral format approved by the election authority.

03
✓

Cast privately

Protect voter choice from disclosure, coercion, tampering and unauthorized access.

04
⌕

Audit the process

Enable independent verification, incident response and lawful recount or dispute procedures.

!

Important: Remote voting creates serious risks, including vote-buying, coercion, identity fraud, malware and loss of ballot secrecy. A pilot must not proceed without legal authorization, independent assessment and a credible fallback.

SAFETY & GOVERNANCE

Security is not a feature.
It is the foundation.

A real election system must withstand technical attacks and real-world pressure. SafeVote proposes a staged, independently reviewed approach before any live use is considered.

See the evaluation roadmap →
✓

Independent penetration testingQualified external assessors, published scope and remediation before any pilot.

✓

Privacy and data protection reviewMinimize personal data and assess applicable Nigerian legal requirements.

✓

Transparent audit and recount planEvidence that results can be checked independently and disputes handled fairly.

✓

Voter safety and accessibility testingEvaluate coercion risks, disability access, digital exclusion and support channels.
